# Code of Conduct

Okal contributors collaborate with professionalism, respect, and technical
clarity.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work and evidence, not the person's ability or intent.
- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Ask when context is missing instead of assuming.
- Disclose conflicts, blockers, mistakes, and security concerns early.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and accessibility.
- Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels.

Review comments use `Must change:`, `Suggestion:`, `Question:`, or `Nit:` so
their intent is clear.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
publishing private information, credential sharing, retaliation, or knowingly
misleading reviewers are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Maintainers may
edit or remove content, limit participation, revoke access, or escalate serious
behavior. Reports should be handled confidentially and without retaliation.
